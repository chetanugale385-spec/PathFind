Startup Strategy Deck

"Bridging the gap between engineering curriculum and industry reality."

The Problem

Engineering students often struggle with "Analysis Paralysis"—having too many resources but no clear direction. This results in missing critical industry skills required for AIML placements by the final year.

The Solution

PathFinder.AI provides a structured, multi-stage roadmap that aligns academic theory with project-based learning. By visualizing the journey, students can track their progress and focus on high-yield skills like Neural Networks and API Deployment.
