The Solution Pitch

"Empowering the next generation of engineers with data-driven clarity."

⚠️

01. The Problem

Academic Fragmentation

Most B.Tech students lack a clear connection between their college syllabus and industry requirements. This gap leads to "Skill Paralysis," where students learn many things but master none.

💡

02. The Solution

PathFinder Engine

A web-based visualizer that maps core AIML competencies directly to academic milestones. It simplifies the 4-year journey into actionable stages, ensuring students focus on high-impact skills first.

🚀

03. Future Vision

Scaling to the Ecosystem

Integrating real-time job market data and personalized SGPA tracking to adjust roadmaps dynamically. The goal is to make PathFinder.AI the standard navigator for engineering colleges in Pune and beyond.
